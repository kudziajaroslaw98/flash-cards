## What are flash cards?

Before we cover this app functionalities it's important for you to understand
**what flash cards are**.

Flash cards are **small cards** with information written on them.
On one side, there is a **question or word**, and on the other side, there is the **answer or explanation**.

You can use them to **learn and remember things**.

For example, you can use flash cards to **study new words**, math facts, or anything else you want to **learn**.

Here are some simple things to remember when creating flash cards:

- **Keep it short and clear**

  Write only the most important information. Don't put too much text on one card.

- **Use simple language**

  Make it easy to understand, especially if you're still learning the topic.

- **Focus on one idea per card**

  Each card should have one question or concept. This helps you stay focused.

- **Use both sides wisely**

  Write the question or term on one side and the answer or explanation on the other.

- **Make them personal**

  Write the cards in a way that makes sense to you. Use your own words or examples.

## Revise

The "Revise" section **leverages spaced repetition** to help you retain
knowledge effectively. This feature prioritizes cards based on how well you
remember them.

### How to Use:

**Start Revising:**

1. **(optional)** Select a set (all flash cards by default) to revise.
2. The app will present cards that needs to be revised.
3. Choose one of the four options:

   - **Again:** The card will reappear in 10 minutes.
   - **Hard:** The card will reappear in 1 hour.
   - **Good:** The card will reappear in 1 day.
   - **Easy:** The card will reappear in 1 week.

   or answers for **Detailed Flash Card** type.

4. After picking the option you will be able to reshuffle to get new random card from the ones that need to be revised.
5. There are differences that you can notice in how the revise behaves for different types of flash cards.

- **Detailed Flash Card:** The card will have it's answer and 3 randomly picked
  answers from cards with the same type.

By default selecting **correct answer** (green background and border on picked answer) will space repetition the card with **'Good'** option. **Wrong answer** (red background and border on picked answer) will behave as **'Hard'** option.

After revising all cards you will be asked to continue revising as it is optional.
If you continue you will revise all cards from the pool.

If you find yourself struggle with a word and picking **'Again'** or **'Hard'** option the **revise date will change** (lets say you picked 'Again' so the date of next revision will change for example from 4 days to 10 minutes) to speed up the process of revising it.

Picking options **'Good'** or **'Easy' won't affect the revise date** to avoid stacking the space repetition multiplier used in the algorithm.

### Best Practices:

- Be honest when rating your recall to optimize the spaced repetition algorithm.
- Aim to review cards daily to build long-term retention.

## Methodology

The Flashcards app is built on **evidence-based learning** techniques to **maximize retention** and understanding:

1. **Spaced Repetition:**
   - Revisit information at increasing intervals to strengthen memory.
2. **Active Recall:**
   - Actively test your knowledge rather than passively rereading material.
3. **Chunking:**
   - Break down large volumes of information into manageable "chunks" (sets).
