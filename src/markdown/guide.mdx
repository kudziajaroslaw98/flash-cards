# Guide to Using the Flashcards App

Welcome to the Flashcards app! This guide will help you get started and explain how to effectively use the app to enhance your learning experience. Below, we cover the main sections of the app and the methodology behind each feature.

---

## **1. Sets**

The "Sets" section is where you organize your flashcards into collections based on topics, subjects, or categories. Think of these as decks of cards that help you focus on specific areas of study.

### How to Use:

- **Create a Set:**
  1. Click the green "+" button.
  2. Name your set and provide a brief description.
  3. Add flashcards by entering a term (front) and its definition or description (back).
- **Search and Filter:**
  - Use the search bar to find specific sets.
  - Filter sets by category to quickly navigate large collections.
- **Edit or Delete:**
  - Use the pencil (edit) and trash (delete) icons to manage your sets.

### Best Practices:

- Create sets based on your study goals, e.g., "Everyday Vocabulary" or "History Terms."
- Keep each set focused to make studying manageable.

---

## **2. Learn**

The "Learn" section is designed to help you study flashcards interactively by presenting terms and testing your recall.

### How to Use:

- **Start Learning:**
  1. Select a set from the "Sets" section.
  2. Review the flashcards one by one.
  3. Try to recall the answer before flipping the card to check.
- **Interactive Mode:**
  - The app will show a question or term (front) and reveal the answer (back) after you attempt to recall it.

### Best Practices:

- Avoid distractions while using this mode to fully engage with the material.
- Focus on understanding the terms rather than just memorizing.

---

## **3. Revise**

The "Revise" section leverages spaced repetition to help you retain knowledge effectively. This feature prioritizes cards based on how well you remember them.

### How to Use:

- **Start Revising:**
  1. Select a set to revise.
  2. The app will present cards based on your learning progress.
  3. Choose one of the four options:
     - **Again:** The card will reappear in 10 minutes.
     - **Hard:** The card will reappear in 1 hour.
     - **Good:** The card will reappear in 1 day.
     - **Easy:** The card will reappear in 1 week.
- **Reshuffle:**
  - Use the "Space" or "Reshuffle" button to randomize the card order.

### Best Practices:

- Be honest when rating your recall to optimize the spaced repetition algorithm.
- Aim to review cards daily to build long-term retention.

---

## **Methodology**

The Flashcards app is built on evidence-based learning techniques to maximize retention and understanding:

1. **Spaced Repetition:**
   - Revisit information at increasing intervals to strengthen memory.
2. **Active Recall:**
   - Actively test your knowledge rather than passively rereading material.
3. **Chunking:**
   - Break down large volumes of information into manageable "chunks" (sets).

---

By following this guide and consistently using the app, you can make learning more efficient and enjoyable. Happy studying!
